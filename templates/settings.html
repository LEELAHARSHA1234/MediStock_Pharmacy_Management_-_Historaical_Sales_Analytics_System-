Technology stack

  • SQLite – local structured data storage

  • CSV export – downloadable reports
  • Historical CSV analytics – interactive bar and pie charts

Important project notes

  • Data is stored locally in data/medistock.db.
  • This is a learning/demo project, not a validated pharmacy or prescription system.
  • Use real medicine data only with appropriate checks and authorization.
